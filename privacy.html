Zen Bun privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Zen Bun is a meditation app with a small game around it. It is made to collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Zen Bun has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (zen, levels, the days you sat, what you bought and your settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. If you delete the app, it is gone.

Apple Health

If you turn on "Apple Health" in the app's settings, each meditation is saved to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. Zen Bun only writes these sessions. It never reads anything from Apple Health. This data stays in Apple Health, under your control, and you can remove the permission at any time in the Health app.

Purchases

Zen Bun Plus, zen bags and tea for the cat are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, card or Apple ID. The app only learns what was bought, so it can deliver it.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved.

Children

Zen Bun collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does anything new with your data.
