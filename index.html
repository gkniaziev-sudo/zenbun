Zen Bun

Meditation for people who keep quitting meditation.

Sit with Bun, a chubby steamed-bun monk, for a few minutes a day. Breathe in and out together and he floats a little higher. Tap him for zen and spend it on tea, teapots, robes and a cosier terrace. Missed a day? Nothing is lost.

Need help? Write to gkniaziev@proton.me. A real person answers, usually within a few days.

Questions

I bought Zen Bun Plus. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Open Settings in the app (the gear next to Places), tap the Zen Bun Plus card, and choose Restore purchases at the bottom. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Is my progress saved if I delete the app?

No. Your progress lives only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it. Zen Bun Plus comes back with Restore purchases; zen bags and tea for the cat are used up once delivered.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why doesn't my phone vibrate during a sit?

Check the Haptics setting in the sheet before you begin (Strong, Soft or Off), and that System Haptics are on in your phone's Settings, under Sounds & Haptics.

Why is there no sound?

Outside a sit, Zen Bun follows the silent switch and stays quiet while your own music plays. A sit's gong and ticks always play. Sounds, music and volume are in the app's Settings.

How do I save my sits to Apple Health?

Turn on Apple Health in the app's Settings and allow Mindful Minutes. Zen Bun only writes your sits; it never reads anything from Health.

The daily reminder doesn't come.

Turn on Daily reminder in the app's Settings and allow notifications. Bun skips days you've already sat.

Zen Bun helps you build a meditation habit. It is not a medical app and doesn't treat any condition.
